📄 Terms & Conditions

1. Purpose

Aviator Analyzer is a historical statistics and analysis tool designed for entertainment and research.

2. No Guaranteed Predictions

The analyzer does not guarantee, predict, or control future Aviator outcomes. Game results may be random.

3. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for their own decisions and should never risk money they cannot afford to lose.

4. Account Information

Users must provide accurate registration information and should never share confidential passwords or financial credentials.

5. Service Changes

Features may be changed, updated, suspended, or removed as the platform develops.

6. Acceptance

By using Aviator Analyzer, you acknowledge that historical statistics cannot guarantee future game results.

🔒 Privacy & Data

Aviator Analyzer collects information submitted during account registration, such as your name, phone number and email address, so the account system can function.

Analysis history may also be stored in your browser and can be exported or backed up by you.

Never enter your M-Pesa PIN, banking password, or other confidential credentials into this website.

This is currently a prototype. Before public production use, stronger authentication, secure password hashing, database security, HTTPS and a complete privacy policy should be implemented.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can Aviator Analyzer predict the next multiplier?

No. Aviator outcomes are random. The analyzer provides historical statistics and risk indicators, not guaranteed predictions.

Does it automatically receive live game results?

The system is prepared for an authorized live-data source, but it does not invent or fake live results.

Can I save my analysis?

Yes. Your analysis can be stored locally, exported, and backed up for later restoration.

Can I create an account?

Yes. You can register with your name, phone number and email address.
